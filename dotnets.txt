## Tenant-Based Workspace Deployment

### How Tenants Work

Your workspace location is automatically determined by your selected tenant:

1. **Tenant Selection**: Choose your tenant from the dropdown (populated from your entitlements)
2. **Automatic Deployment**: Workspace deploys to the infrastructure associated with that tenant
3. **Resource Isolation**: Each tenant has dedicated resources and configurations

<details>
summary><strong>Available Tenants</strong></summary>

| Tenant | Purpose | Infrastructure Location |
|--------|---------|-------------------------|
| **default** | General development | Primary AWS region |
| **region-*** | Regional-specific work | Corresponding AWS region |
| **tenant1** | Specialized team | Dedicated infrastructure |
| **tenant2** | Another specialized team | Dedicated infrastructure |

> **Note**: Your available tenants are determined by your SES entitlements. Contact your manager if you need access to additional tenants.
</details>

### Tenant Benefits

- **Consistent Environments**: Same tools and configurations across your team
- **Data Sovereignty**: Workspaces deploy to appropriate regions based on data requirements
- **Resource Optimization**: Efficient use of shared infrastructure
- **Security Boundaries**: Isolated environments for different business units
