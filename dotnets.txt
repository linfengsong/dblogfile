
### Automatic Capability Assignment

Based on your entitlements, capabilities are automatically injected into your workspace:

| Capability | Description | How It's Added |
|------------|-------------|----------------|
| **AI** | AI/ML tooling and GPU access | Based on AI entitlement |
| **Advanced Analytics** | Additional data science tools | Based on analytics role |
| **Security Tools** | Security scanning and analysis | Based on security clearance |
| **Compliance Tools** | Regulatory compliance tooling | Based on compliance requirements |

<details>
<summary><strong>How Capability Injection Works</strong></summary>

1. **Entitlement Check**: During workspace creation, RDX checks your SES entitlements
2. **Capability Mapping**: Maps entitlements to specific capabilities
3. **Tool Injection**: Appropriate tools and configurations are added to your workspace
4. **Resource Adjustment**: Additional resources (like GPUs) are allocated if needed

**Example**: If you have "AI" entitlement, your workspace automatically gets:
- NVIDIA GPU access
- AI/ML frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch)
- Model training tools
- Additional memory allocation
</details>

## SES Entitlements Integration

### Entitlement-Based Access

Your template access and capabilities are determined by SES (Service Entitlement System):

<details>
<summary><strong>Default Entitlements</strong></summary>

All users approved for RDX access automatically receive:
- **RDX-General template** within their default tenant
- **Basic capabilities** (standard development tools)

**Specialized templates require additional entitlements:**
- **RDX-Analysis**: Requires "Analytics" entitlement
- **Specialized tenants**: Require specific team/role entitlements

**Request Process:** Additional entitlements can be requested through the Request Portal.
</details>

### Template ID Format

Templates are identified using the format: `${tenant}_${template}`

**Examples:**
- `default_rdx-general` - General template in default tenant
- `tenant1_elastic` - Elastic template in tenant1
- `region-us-east-1_rdx-analysis` - Analysis template in US East region
