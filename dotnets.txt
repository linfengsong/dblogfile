# RDX Template System: User Guide

## Introduction to RDX Templates

RDX templates provide standardized, on-demand development environments tailored to different business needs. Instead of choosing a template based on programming language, you now select templates based on your work function—making it easier to get the right tools for your specific task.

<details>
<summary><strong>What's New in the Template System</strong></summary>

- **Business-Focused Selection**: Choose templates based on what you need to do (general development, analytics, etc.) rather than technical stack
- **Multi-Language Support**: All programming languages (Python, Java, Go, .NET) available in every workspace
- **Tenant-Based Deployment**: Workspace location automatically determined by your team/tenant
- **Capability Injection**: AI and other capabilities automatically added based on your entitlements
- **Simplified Experience**: Reduced from 8 separate templates to 3 focused options
</details>

## Available Templates

### RDX-General Template
**Purpose**: General-purpose development for any programming language

**Best For**: Software development, prototyping, multi-language projects, team collaboration

**Includes**:
- Python, Java, Go, .NET toolchains
- VS Code and IntelliJ IDE support
- JupyterLab for notebooks
- Git integration with SSH key setup
- Standard development tools and libraries

**Resource Allocation**: Configurable CPU, memory, and storage based on your needs

### RDX-Analysis Template
**Purpose**: Specialized workspace for data analytics and data science

**Best For**: Data analysis, machine learning, statistical modeling, business intelligence

**Includes**:
- Everything in RDX-General plus:
- Conda environment management
- Additional data science libraries (pandas, numpy, scikit-learn, etc.)
- Analytics-specific tooling
- Pre-configured Jupyter environments

**Resource Allocation**: Optimized for data-intensive workloads
