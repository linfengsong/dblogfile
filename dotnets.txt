### Cost Management

- **Use Deploy-Self-Service** for cost-sensitive operations
- **Set appropriate TTL** to auto-stop inactive workspaces
- **Monitor usage patterns** and adjust resource allocations
- **Delete unused workspaces** regularly
- **Leverage tenant-based resource pooling** for team efficiency

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: Can I switch templates after creating a workspace?</strong></summary>

**A:** No, templates cannot be changed after workspace creation. You would need to create a new workspace with the desired template and migrate your work. However, you can adjust resources and some parameters on existing workspaces.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: What happens if I select the wrong tenant?</strong></summary>

**A:** Your workspace will deploy to the infrastructure associated with that tenant. If you need a different tenant, you'll need to create a new workspace. Contact support if you need assistance with tenant selection.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: How do I get access to specialized templates like RDX-Analysis?</strong></summary>

**A:** Specialized templates require additional entitlements. Submit a request through the Request Portal, and your manager will review and approve based on your role requirements.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: Can I customize a template for my team's specific needs?</strong></summary>

**A:** Yes, teams can request custom template configurations through the RDX engineering team. Common customizations include pre-installed packages, specific tool versions, or custom environment configurations.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: How are template updates handled?</strong></summary>

**A:** Template updates are managed by the RDX engineering team through CI/CD pipelines. Updates are automatically deployed to development environments for testing before promotion to production. Existing workspaces continue with their current versions until recreated.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: What's the difference between tenants and templates?</strong></summary>

**A:** Tenants determine **where** your workspace runs (which infrastructure/region), while templates determine **what** tools and configurations are available in your workspace. You select both during workspace creation.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: How do capabilities differ from templates?</strong></summary>

**A:** Templates provide the base environment (like RDX-General or RDX-Analysis), while capabilities are additional features injected based on your entitlements (like AI tools, security scanning, etc.). Think of templates as the vehicle and capabilities as the optional features added to that vehicle.
</details>
